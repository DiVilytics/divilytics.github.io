F.A.Q. General Gameplay & Core Mechanics

Turn InitiationA turn is officially in progress once the mover is placed in a new location.
Retracting MovesMovement can be retracted only if no actions have been performed at the new location.
Condition Card ExecutionConditions cannot be played unless the player can perform the exact action described in the text.
Discard-Based TriggersConditions triggered by an opponent discarding cards apply only to cards discarded from the hand via actions or effects.
Zero FloorsNo game values (Power, Strength, etc.) can drop below zero.
Locked LocationsCards cannot be played to, moved to, or activated within locked locations.
Search Protocol ("Find")When instructed to find a card, search the Realm first, then the discard pile, then the deck.
VerificationWhen retrieving a specific category of card (e.g., Ally) from a deck/discard pile, the card must be shown to opponents for verification.
Infinite Search LoopsIf a card requires searching for a specific type that no longer exists in the deck/discard pile, play the card solely to reshuffle the deck and discard pile.
Zero-Strength HeroesThese may be defeated via a Vanquish action without using Allies. If Allies are used, they are not discarded.
